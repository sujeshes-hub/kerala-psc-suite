13.2 Tenses (കാലങ്ങൾ)
🖼 Fig. 1 — 12 Tenses Table
4 × 3 = 12 tenses: Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous × Present, Past, Future.

Simple Present — ഉപയോഗം

  • ശീലം, പതിവ് (habit): I wake up at 6 am.
  • സത്യം (universal truth): The sun rises in the east.
  • ഭാവി പ്രവർത്തനം (scheduled): The train leaves at 9 am.

Present Perfect — ഉപയോഗം

  • just, already, yet, ever, never, so far, recently, lately കൂടെ.
  • Structure: has/have + past participle.
  • ഉദാ: I have finished my homework.

Past Perfect — ഉപയോഗം

  • രണ്ട് past actions-ൽ ആദ്യത്തേത്.
  • Structure: had + past participle.
  • ഉദാ: He had left before I arrived.
🧠 "SP-CP-Perf-PC" — Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous.
13.3 Articles (a/an/the)

Rules

Articleഉപയോഗംഉദാഹരണം
aConsonant sound-ൽ തുടങ്ങുന്ന ഏകവചനംa book, a university, a one-rupee coin
anVowel sound-ൽ തുടങ്ങുന്ന ഏകവചനംan apple, an hour, an MLA, an honest man
theനിർദ്ദിഷ്ടമായ (specific) കാര്യംthe sun, the Ganga, the best

a vs an — Trick

⚡ Sound നോക്കണം, അക്ഷരമല്ല!
• a university (യൂ-നിവേഴ്സിറ്റി — consonant sound)
• an hour (ഔവർ — vowel sound)
• a European (യൂറോപ്യൻ)
• an honest man (ഓണസ്റ്റ് — vowel sound)
• a one-rupee coin (വൺ — consonant sound)

'the' ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്ന സന്ദർഭങ്ങൾ

  • അദ്വിതീയ വസ്തുക്കൾ: the sun, the moon, the earth
  • നദികൾ, കടലുകൾ, പർവ്വതനിരകൾ: the Nile, the Arabian Sea, the Himalayas
  • ഗ്രന്ഥങ്ങൾ: the Ramayana, the Bible
  • Superlatives: the best, the tallest
  • Ordinals: the first, the second
  • ഒരു ജാതി മുഴുവൻ: the rich, the poor

'the' ഉപയോഗിക്കാത്ത സന്ദർഭങ്ങൾ

  • ഭാഷകൾ: English, Malayalam
  • കായിക വിനോദങ്ങൾ: cricket, football
  • ഭക്ഷണം: rice, bread
  • രാജ്യങ്ങൾ (ഏകവചനം): India, France
⚠️ PSC Trap
a university (sound = "yu") — consonant sound.
an hour (sound = "our") — vowel sound.
അക്ഷരമല്ല, ഉച്ചാരണം നോക്കണം!

13.5 Voice (Active & Passive)

Active vs Passive

Active VoicePassive Voice
Subject പ്രവർത്തനം ചെയ്യുന്നുSubject-നോട് പ്രവർത്തനം ചെയ്യപ്പെടുന്നു
Ravi wrote the letter.The letter was written by Ravi.
She is cooking food.Food is being cooked by her.
They will complete the project.The project will be completed by them.

Passive Voice — രൂപീകരണം

⚡ Passive = Object + Helping Verb + Past Participle + by + Subject

Simple Present: is/am/are + V3
Present Continuous: is/am/are being + V3
Present Perfect: has/have been + V3
Simple Past: was/were + V3
Past Continuous: was/were being + V3
Past Perfect: had been + V3
Simple Future: will be + V3
Future Perfect: will have been + V3

ഉദാഹരണങ്ങൾ

  • Active: He reads a book. → Passive: A book is read by him.
  • Active: She has written a letter. → Passive: A letter has been written by her.
  • Active: They will help us. → Passive: We will be helped by them.
⚠️ PSC Trap
Passive-ൽ tense മാറില്ല. Active "is writing" → Passive "is being written" (Present Continuous തന്നെ).
Intransitive verbs (sleep, come, go) — Passive രൂപീകരിക്കാൻ കഴിയില്ല.

Reporting Verb Changes

DirectIndirect
saidsaid (statement)

ഉദാഹരണങ്ങൾ

  • Direct: He said, "I am busy." → Indirect: He said that he was busy.
  • Direct: She said to me, "Will you help me?" → Indirect: She asked me if I would help her.
  • Direct: He said, "What is your name?" → Indirect: He asked what my name was.
🧠 "SO-NT-NP" — SAY → SAID, SAID TO → TOLD, QUESTION → ASKED.
"SO = Said only, NT = No Tense change (for universal truths), NP = New Pronoun."

13.11 Sentence Correction (വാക്യ ശുദ്ധി)

Common Errors — ഉദാഹരണങ്ങൾ

❌ തെറ്റ്✅ ശരി
He is senior than me.He is senior to me.
One of my friendOne of my friends
I have been living here since 5 years.I have been living here for 5 years.
He is one of the best student.He is one of the best students.
The furnitures are new.The furniture is new.
Return back my book.Return my book.
Discuss about the matter.Discuss the matter.
He is more taller than me.He is taller than I am.
I have bought a new pair of shoes.✔ (Correct)
Each of the boys are here.Each of the boys is here.

ശ്രദ്ധിക്കേണ്ട കാര്യങ്ങൾ

  • Senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior — ഇവയ്ക്ക് ശേഷം to (than അല്ല).
  • One of — എപ്പോഴും ബഹുവചനം.
  • Furniture, information, advice, luggage, machinery — Uncountable nouns (s ചേർക്കരുത്).
  • Each, every, either, neither — ഏകവചന ക്രിയ.
  • since = point of time; for = period of time.
⚠️ PSC Trap
Senior/Junior/Superior/Inferior + to (not "than").
Discuss — "about" ഇല്ല (Discuss about = ❌).
Return — "back" ഇല്ല (Return back = ❌).
